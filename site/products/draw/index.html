1Drawing

Shapes, colour and words, where you want them.

Shapes, pens, brushes and text, gradients and shadows, layers you can name and lock, and a library of diagrams. Spelling is checked in every text layer, privately.

2Animating

Make anything move.

Every layer can move, turn, fade and change colour along a timeline: keyframes with easing, particles and sound. Build a chart up, bring a logo to life, or swing a ball round an arc with two keyframes.

3Presenting

Then present it as slides.

Add slides to any canvas, choose how each one arrives (a fade, a push, a clock-hand sweep), write speaker notes and present full screen. Or export the whole thing as a video.
