Xenify Server

Bring your team together, on a computer you own.

The server is what turns the apps into one place for your whole team: a shared wiki, editing together, chat, email and calendars, plans across every team, and the apps in any browser. It's free, and it runs at home, in the office or on a server you rent. We never see your data.

Install it with Xenify Hub

What your team gets.

WikiEverything in one searchable placeSpaces for each team or project, holding documents, spreadsheets, plans and drawings. Search finds the words inside them. More about the wiki
TogetherEdit at the same timeWork in the same document, spreadsheet, plan or drawing at once, see where everyone is, and leave comments. Every version is kept, and changes made offline are sent when you're back.
Chat and emailMessages, email and calendars for everyoneChats, video calls and meetings, everyone's email next to their chats, shared calendars, contacts and room bookings. Connect your company's email once and it's there for everyone. More about Chat
PlanEvery team's plans in one placeThe server works out every plan's dates and everyone's time across all of them, around holidays and days off, so managers see the whole picture. More about Plan
BrowserThe apps in any browserWrite, Calc, Plan, Draw and Chat open in a browser from your server, with nothing to install. Share a read-only link to a page with someone outside, and stop it any time.
AIAI for everyone, kept in-houseRun AI models on your own graphics card, or use a provider you trust. Everyone uses them in every app without a key of their own. What hardware you need to run AI locally
DataSpreadsheets linked to your databasesConnect your databases once; people pick a table and work with it as a sheet that stays current, or edit it.

And it looks after itself.

YoursYour files stay with youEverything is on a computer you own, over secure connections. Nothing goes through us. On Linux its files can be encrypted on disk too.
Sign-inSimple to join, hard to break intoAdmins add people, who get an email to pick their password. Two-step sign-in for everyone or just admins.
BackupsDaily backupsTo a second disk, an SFTP server or cloud storage, encrypted with your passphrase, and deleted files wait in a bin for 30 days.
UpdatesEvery computer kept up to dateChoose which versions your team's computers get and when. The server updates itself without going offline.
