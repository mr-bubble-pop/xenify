AI in Xenify

AI on a graphics card you own.

Check a whole document, ask about it, and make pictures and short clips. Run the models on your own graphics card through Xenify Server, or use a provider you trust. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose where.

Set it upWhat hardware you need to run AI locally
AI is optional, and off until you turn it on.

Nothing is sent anywhere for AI unless you set it up. There are two ways to have it: pay a provider such as OpenRouter with your own key, or run the models yourself on hardware you own. This page is about running them yourself. How to set up either

WriteCheck the whole documentBeyond the spelling and grammar checker that always runs on your computer: the AI reads the document and suggests fixes you accept or ignore. Ask it to summarise, rewrite or explain.
DrawMake and change picturesDescribe a picture and it's made for you, or paint over part of one and say what should change there.
Calc and PlanAsk about your sheets and plansAsk questions about a spreadsheet or a plan in plain words. In Calc, the AI asks how you work things out before it changes anything.
Your choiceOff until you pick where it runsYour team's server with its own graphics card, or a provider such as OpenRouter with your own key. People on a server use its AI with no key of their own.

Hardware

What hardware you need to run AI locally.

We ran the same tests on three graphics cards. Times are once the model is loaded, made the same way on every card. More graphics memory lets you run bigger models; a faster card makes the same thing sooner.

16 GB cardTested on a Tesla V100
  • Write's AI check with Gemma 4 12B
  • Pictures in 4 to 16 seconds
  • Clips, slowly: about half an hour each
24 GB cardTested on an RTX 3090
  • Gemma 4 26B, the best checker we tested
  • Pictures in 4 to 13 seconds
  • Clips in about 20 minutes
48 GB cardTested on an RTX A6000
  • Every model here, with room to spare
  • Pictures in 3 to 9 seconds
  • Clips in about 12 minutes

Qwen3.5 9B30 s19 s24 s70%95%4%

Seconds to check 1,000 words. Each model was set to answer straight away instead of reasoning step by step first, which is much faster. Mistakes found and right fixes were judged by two other AI models; a false alarm is a correct sentence marked as wrong. A model too big for the card can still run with part of it in the computer's memory, much more slowly: Gemma 4 26B took 89 s per 1,000 words on the 16 GB card. We haven't timed a 12 GB card yet.
