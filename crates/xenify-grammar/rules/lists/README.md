# Register word lists

Words and phrases that mark informal or register-specific writing. They are
not mistakes; a writing profile decides whether to allow, note or flag them.

Format: one entry per line, `word or phrase | standard English equivalent`
(the equivalent may be empty). Lines starting with `#` are comments. Matching
ignores case and curly apostrophes.

These lists were written for Xenify Write from general knowledge of English
usage (no list was copied from another product or dictionary). MIT licence,
like the rest of the project.
