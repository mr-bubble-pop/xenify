Checkout redesign

OwnerPriya Shah, ProductStatusIn review
Version0.4 · 18 October 2026Sign-offHead of Product, CTO

1 Purpose

Customers leave the basket at the payment step more often than at any other point. This project will streamline checkout to a single page and make use of saved cards, so that returning customers can pay in under thirty seconds.

2 Scope

In scope: the basket, delivery and payment steps on web and the mobile app. Out of scope: the order history page and reciepts.
